# Spec: UI polish after review (MY-165)

Decisions in force: `theme-three-states` (the theme is revealed as a circle from the button, skipped
under reduced motion or without View Transitions), `design-tokens` and `plain-css-and-tokens` (colours,
sizes and durations only from tokens), `ui-dependencies` (the own toast: one at a time,
`role="status"`, 5 seconds, paused while hovered or focused), `base-select-pickers` (the pills are
native selects), `list-animations` (what enters uses `@starting-style`), `accessibility-and-seo` (WCAG
2.2 AA, both themes) and `js-budget-90-kb`. The edit form of `edit-delete` listed "a visible Save or
Cancel button" as out of scope; this spec reverses that (decision `edit-form-buttons`). The status
filter, the counter and the footer (MY-154) and list animation (MY-158) are other tasks.

## Theme change

The circle takes about 500 ms and eases in and out, both read from tokens. With
`prefers-reduced-motion` or without View Transitions the theme still changes at once.

## Hover and selected states

A change of colour is no longer a jump. On every button and pill that changes look on hover, and on
the theme chip that becomes the selected one, the background and the colour change over
`--duration-base`:

- the three theme chips (hover, and the chip that becomes selected, with its icon);
- the row's pencil and trash, and the edit form's two new buttons (the icon colour);
- the pills (hover), the "Add task" button (hover) and the toast's action button (hover).

Hover stays under `@media (hover: hover)`, so a tap leaves no hover look. With
`prefers-reduced-motion` none of these animate. The task row's own hover fill, the checkbox and the
open list of a pill (already faded in) are unchanged.

## The row's buttons

The pencil and the trash sit at the vertical centre of the row, not level with the title. When the
buttons do not fit beside the title (320px, large text) they still wrap onto their own line at the
right, as now.

## Tooltips

Hovering a theme chip, the pencil, the trash, the edit form's two buttons or a pill (category or
priority) shows the browser's tooltip with the same text as the name a screen reader hears
("Light theme", "Edit <title>", "Delete <title>", "Save", "Cancel", "Category", "Priority"). The
accessibility tree shows that name once and no description: the tooltip is the `title` attribute, placed
where the browser makes no second description of it (the plan says where). No tooltip of our own.

## The toast

- A new toast fades in and slides up from below over `--duration-base`.
- When it goes (after 5 seconds, or because its button was used), it fades out and slides down the same
  way, then leaves the page. While it leaves it takes no pointer or keyboard input and Ctrl/Cmd+Z does
  nothing: it counts as gone.
- A new toast replaces a leaving one at once, with no overlap.
- The pause on hover and focus, the 5 seconds, the action and Ctrl/Cmd+Z of a toast that is shown are as
  before.
- With `prefers-reduced-motion` it appears and goes at once.

## The edit form

At the end of the form, on the right where the row's buttons are, two icon buttons, each at least 44px:

| Button | Icon    | Name     | Does                                      |
| ------ | ------- | -------- | ----------------------------------------- |
| Save   | `Check` | "Save"   | saves like Enter in the title             |
| Cancel | `X`     | "Cancel" | closes and keeps the old values, like Esc |

- Both close the form and move the focus to the row's pencil, as Enter and Esc do.
- Pressing either with the mouse or a finger keeps the focus where it was in the form, so it is never
  "leaving the form"; Cancel never saves, in any browser.
- Tab order in the form: the title, the category, the priority, Save, Cancel. Space and Enter on a button
  press it. Enter in the title still saves, Esc still cancels, an empty title still keeps the old one,
  and the focus leaving the form still saves.
- On a phone a task's edit can now be cancelled.

## Width

`--content-max-width` goes from 35rem to 42rem: the header, the page and the toast are wider. Under
480px nothing changes, and at 320px there is no horizontal scroll.

## Out of scope

The task row's hover fill, a pressed (`:active`) look of the buttons, the checkbox, a tooltip of our own,
a different toast position or length, the status filter, the counter, the footer and list animations.
