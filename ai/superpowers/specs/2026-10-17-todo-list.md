# Spec: the task list and the completion checkbox (MY-152)

Decisions in force: `todo-model` (the fields; completed tasks sink below the active ones, newest
first), `design-tokens` and `plain-css-and-tokens` (colours, spacing and sizes only from tokens),
`ui-dependencies` (`lucide-react` icons), `accessibility-and-seo` (WCAG 2.2 AA, both themes),
`structure-and-naming` and `context-folders` (where the files go). The form and the header are
another task, so the list is the only content of the page; editing, deleting, the toast that shows
`error`, the filters, the counter and the sort control come later, and so does list animation
(`list-animations`).

## What the page shows

Under the content-width column the tasks area is one of three things, never two at once:

| Situation                                     | The area shows                                     |
| --------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------- |
| the first read has not finished (`isLoading`) | the text "Loading tasks…"                          |
| loaded, no tasks                              | the text "No tasks yet. Add your first one above." |
| loaded, at least one task                     | the list, one row per task                         |

- A failed first read leaves the list empty, so the area shows the empty text. The list never shows
  the failure itself: the context holds the message in `error` and the toast of a later task shows
  it, the same way it shows a failed write.
- The three share one look: a `--color-surface` card with a `--radius-*` corner, no border. The two
  texts are muted and centred; neither is a live region (the toast owns the announcements).

## A row

Left to right and top to bottom:

1. A checkbox, checked when the task is completed.
2. The title, wrapped as many lines as it needs.
3. Below the title, indented to its left edge: the category (a dot in the category colour, then its
   label: Work, Personal, Study, Shopping, Health) and the priority (a flag icon in the priority
   colour, then its label: Low, Medium, High). Both labels use the muted text colour, so nothing
   rests on a colour alone.

- The row is a list item of a `<ul>` with dividers (`--color-border`) between rows, not above the
  first or below the last.
- The checkbox is outlined with `--color-border-control`, filled with the accent colour and shows a
  check mark when checked.
- A completed task has its title struck through and muted (`--color-text-muted`); the category and
  the priority look the same as on an active task.

## Order

`sortTodos(todos)` (a pure function in `utils/`) gives the order, computed during render:

- Active tasks first, completed tasks after them.
- Inside each group, the newest first by `createdAt`; equal times keep their stored order.
- The input list is not changed. There is no option parameter yet.

A task that changes state moves to its group at once; after a reload it is still in that group.

## Toggling

- A click on the checkbox or on the title, or Space with the checkbox focused, toggles the task
  through `toggleTodo(id)` of `useTodos()`. A click on the category or the priority does nothing.
- The row changes at once (the context is optimistic) and the change is stored. A failed write
  puts the row back; the message waits in `error` for the toast.

## What a screen reader gets

- The checkbox's name is the title, and the label is the title only. Its state is checked or not
  checked.
- The category and the priority belong to the same row: they follow the title in reading order,
  and the checkbox is described by them ("Personal High"), so a user who tabs from checkbox to
  checkbox hears them too. A screen reader hears the words the screen shows; the flag icon and the
  colour dot are hidden from assistive technology.
- The tasks are one list, so the screen reader says how many items it has.

## Layout and keyboard

- Tab reaches each checkbox in the visual order; the focus ring is the global one.
- The row's target (the label with the checkbox and the title) is at least `--touch-target` tall
  and as wide as the row; nothing else in the row is clickable.
- A long title, even one without spaces, wraps inside the row at 320px and never widens the page;
  there is no horizontal scroll from 320px wide to desktop and at 200% zoom.
- Hover feedback (a `--color-surface-hover` row) appears only where the device can hover.
- Both themes work through the tokens alone; contrast is as `design-tokens` fixed it.

## Out of scope

The creation form, the header, edit, delete and Undo, the toast, filters, the counter, mass
actions, the sort control, animation, and any text for a failed load.
