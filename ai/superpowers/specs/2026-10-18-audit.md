## Forced colors: out of scope

A3 to A5 are real in Chrome's forced-colors emulation, but the author decided, after the audit, that Windows contrast
themes are not tuned. Every `forced-colors` rule is removed from `src`, `ai/rules/scope.md` says it is out of scope,
and the style rule that asked for system colours is gone. Old records are not edited: `accessibility-and-seo` still
lists Windows contrast themes among the audit's checks, and the scope line overrides it; the archived plans and specs
that mention forced colours describe what was built then.

## Questions for the author, and what was decided

These changed behaviour or needed a choice. The author answered each; the answer is built or recorded as below.

- **Q1, status switch below 302px.** Three equal columns cannot hold "Completed" (83.52px) from 290px down. Options
  were: one row with columns as wide as their label; the options stacked; as it was. **Decided: columns by label.**
  The switch is one row down to 240px, the columns are equal while there is room and a column never gets narrower
  than its label or 44px. Where the labels cannot fit at all (below 240px, or 150% to 200% text at 320px) it wraps
  rather than scrolls. Built in the reflow commit with A8 and A9.
- **Q2, Undo time limit (2.2.1, level A).** The toast leaves after 5 seconds, paused only while the pointer or the
  focus is inside it. That is none of the six allowed cases (turn off, adjust, extend with a warning, real time,
  essential, 20 hours), and a keyboard user needs about 8 tabs to reach Undo; Ctrl/Cmd+Z works while the toast is
  up and is exposed through `aria-keyshortcuts`. **Decided: keep 5 seconds with the pause, and record it** as the
  decision `undo-toast-time-limit`, a conscious deviation from 2.2.1. No code changed.
- **Q3, speech while typing in the search.** Every character is a filter change, and the live region repeated "N
  tasks shown" per key (typing `the` queued it three times). **Decided: do not repeat the same text.** The result
  speaks when the filters change and its text differs from the last one; the first change after a load still
  speaks, typing that keeps the count and a status or pill change that keeps it are quiet. Built in
  `useFilterAnnouncement`, with no timer; the wording of the rule in `react.md` and the recipe in `feature.md`
  follows. From the live-region log, not from listening: NVDA is unverified.
- **Q4, `replaceState` in Safari's engine.** WebKit 27.2 throws `SecurityError: Attempt to use history.replaceState()
more than 100 times per 10 seconds`; typing 175 characters at once keeps 100 of them, because the field shows the
  URL's `q`. Firefox and Chrome do not limit. **Decided: keep it.** It is a known, accepted limitation: about 100
  calls per 10 seconds, which a person reaches only by holding a key. No code changed.

## Checked, no finding

- Tab order is the visual order everywhere (theme, add form, search, pills, Reset, status, rows, footer, backup,
  toast last); focus is never lost after add (stays in the field), edit (the pencil), Save, Cancel, toggle (the
  checkbox, also when the row leaves a filter), delete and Undo, Clear completed and its Undo, mark all, Reset
  (the Category pill), import. Same in Firefox and WebKit.
- Names: every control has one; the accessibility tree lists no description equal to a name; the checkbox
  description is category and priority ("Work High"); the add form, the edit form and the filters each have a
  "Category" pill, told apart by their value and place.
- Speech from the live regions: add "Task added" and the counter; an empty title the error and `aria-invalid`;
  toggle, delete and Undo the counter; delete "Task deleted Undo"; filters "N tasks shown" or "No tasks match your
  filters"; Reset once; nothing on load.
- Touch targets: every control is at least 44×44 (1000px, 335px, 740×360, with the edit form open).
- Reduced motion: every transition is 0s, no View Transition starts for the theme, the toast leaves at once.
- Text spacing (1.4.12), WCAG zoom 200% and 400% as 640px and 320px at 100% text: no overflow, no clipped text,
  except A11. Phone landscape 740×360: no overflow, the picker opens above the pill and scrolls.
- Contrast of every other state, both themes: placeholder 5.76 and 6.36, completed rows 4.89 and 5.69 on hover,
  search highlight 14.23 and 4.94, hovered text button 4.56, focus ring at least 4.56, error text 4.83 and 6.40.
- `search-shortcut-key` is accurate: the handler ignores Ctrl, Cmd and Alt and the editable fields (the same
  selector as the undo shortcut), prevents the default and only moves the focus. It does fire from a button, a
  checkbox or a radio, as the record says ("always active"). Nothing to change.
- Firefox 157 and WebKit 27.2: the list, the transitions and the new-row fade run, `@starting-style` and nesting
  work, the pills are native selects in Firefox and `base-select` in WebKit (its list and marks render), `/`,
  Escape and Ctrl+Z work.
