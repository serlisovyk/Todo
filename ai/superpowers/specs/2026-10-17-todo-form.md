# Spec: the app header and the task form (MY-151)

Decisions in force: `todo-model` (the fields, the 200-character limit, the fixed categories),
`ui-dependencies` (`lucide-react` icons), `design-tokens` and `plain-css-and-tokens` (colours, scales,
two borders), `accessibility-and-seo` (WCAG 2.2 AA), `context-folders` and `structure-and-naming`
(where the files go), and `native-selects`, written in this task. The task list, the item rows, the
toast and the theme switch are later tasks; this one is the first thing a user sees and the first
caller of `useTodos().addTodo`.

## The page

`App` renders a column at the content width: the header, then `<main>` with the form on a card. The
header sits above `<main>`, not inside it. The task list, a task of its own, follows the form in
`<main>`, and no placeholder stands in for what comes later.

## The header

A `<header>` with a decorative `ListChecks` icon and an `<h1>` with the text "Todo". The icon is
hidden from assistive technology. The header is the page's banner landmark: it is a sibling of
`<main>`, and both are centred at the content width with the same side spacing. The theme switch is
added to this header by a later task.

## The form

Three fields and a button, in this order:

| Control  | Is                                                 | Name for assistive technology |
| -------- | -------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------- |
| Title    | a text field, placeholder "What needs to be done?" | "Task title" (not visible)    |
| Category | a native `<select>` in a pill with a coloured dot  | "Category" (not visible)      |
| Priority | a native `<select>` in a pill with an outline flag | "Priority" (not visible)      |
| Add task | a submit button with a `Plus` icon, text visible   | "Add task"                    |

- The title accepts at most 200 characters (`TITLE_MAX_LENGTH`); a longer paste is cut.
- The category options are Work, Personal, Study, Shopping, Health, the priority options Low, Medium,
  High, in the order of `CATEGORIES` and `PRIORITIES`. The dot has the colour of the selected
  category, the flag the colour of the selected priority; both change at once with the selection.
- At first the field is empty, the category is Work and the priority is Medium. The last choice lives
  only in the form: a reload brings the defaults back, and nothing of the form is stored.
- The button is always enabled. Enter in the title field submits, so does the button, with a mouse,
  a key or a touch. The selects never submit by themselves.

## Submitting

| What happens                          | Result                                                                                                                                                              |
| ------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| The title is empty or only spaces     | Nothing is added and `addTodo` is not called. "Enter a task title." appears under the field and is announced. The field is marked invalid. Focus goes to the field. |
| The title is valid, the task is saved | The field is emptied, focus is in the field, category and priority keep their values. "Task added" is announced.                                                    |
| `addTodo` resolves `null`             | The text stays in the field and focus is in the field. The form says nothing: the failure is in `error` of the context, and the toast of a later task shows it.     |

- `addTodo({ title, category, priority })` gets the three values as submitted; the repository trims
  the title. The task survives a reload because the repository stores it.
- After any submit focus is in the title field, whether Enter or the button sent it. The text of a
  blocked or failed submit is never lost.
- The message belongs to the last submit: it disappears, with the invalid mark and the announcement
  text, as soon as the title changes. Changing a select leaves it alone.
- A second submit with the same result is announced again, so pressing Enter twice on an empty field
  is heard twice.

## Screen readers and keyboard

- One polite live region, `role="status"`, inside the form and hidden with `.sr-only`. It carries
  "Task added" and the error message; the visible error is not a second live region.
- The invalid field has `aria-invalid="true"` and `aria-describedby` pointing at the visible message;
  both are gone when the message is.
- The page has two landmarks: a banner with the `<h1>`, and a main with the form.
- Every control is reachable by Tab in the visual order and has a visible focus ring from the base
  styles. Opening a select and choosing by keyboard works as in any native select.

## Layout

- The form is a card: `--color-surface`, a `--color-border` outline, `--radius-lg`, padding from the
  `--space-*` scale. Every control is at least `--touch-target` tall and outlined with
  `--color-border-control`; the invalid field is outlined in the error colour.
- Wider than 480px: the title takes the first row alone, the message under it; the two selects and
  the button follow on the second row, the button at the end.
- Under 480px (`width < 480px`) the button spans the width and the card padding shrinks. The two
  selects share a row while both fit and take a full row each, stretched, when they do not. From 320px
  up nothing scrolls horizontally, at 200% zoom too; a select cuts a long text with an ellipsis
  instead of overflowing.
- Both themes use the tokens only; text is at least 4.5:1, control borders and the dot and flag at
  least 3:1.

## Out of scope

The task list and its rows, editing, deleting, filters and search, the toast and any display of the
context's `error`, the theme switch, animation, export and import.
